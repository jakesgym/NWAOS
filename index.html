🌐 PLAY WITH FRIENDS (TEST)

One person taps HOST. Everyone else taps JOIN on their game. The host picks how many computer players to add, then taps START.

MOVE: arrow keys / WASD, or the joystick. PUSH: SPACE (or J): shoves whoever is in front of you the way you're facing. SPRINT: hold SHIFT (or K). Touch the lava ball and you POP! The wall closes in every 30 seconds. Last one standing wins.
