SEP Foundation - iPad / home-screen version

1. Put this whole folder on any website host (GitHub Pages, Netlify, etc.).
   It must be served over https.
2. On the iPad, open the site in Safari.
3. Tap Share -> Add to Home Screen.
4. Open it from the home screen: it runs full screen and works offline after the first load.

Sign in with your nwaos.com account (on the game's menu) to save progress to your account
and play online. Without signing in, progress is saved on each device separately.
